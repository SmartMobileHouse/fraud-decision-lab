# Data licenses

This repository's **code and its original synthetic data** are MIT licensed
([LICENSE](LICENSE)). **No third-party dataset is included, and MIT does not
license any of them.** You download external datasets yourself into
`data/external/` (gitignored) and you are responsible for following their
terms. This page summarises our reading of those terms; it is not legal advice.

Each dataset config declares `external_processing_allowed`. When it is
`false`, the lab refuses to send that dataset's rows to any AI provider,
whatever the CLI flags say.

Publication is a separate permission: `publication_allowed` defaults to `false`,
and `publication_note` records the approval basis. HTML reports and JSON evidence
exports require approval. A processing permission does not authorize redistribution
of rows, predictions, aggregate metrics, plots or reports. In the shipped configs,
the bundled synthetic dataset and ULB aggregate results are approved; IEEE-CIS is not. These flags record
a review decision; changing them does not establish legal rights.
